## FAST Radio Telescope (Pingtang County, Guizhou)

The Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope (FAST), nicknamed "Tianyan", is a radio telescope located in the Dawodang depression, a natural karst basin in Pingtang County, Guizhou. It is the world's largest single-dish radio telescope, with a dish diameter of 500 meters. Construction began in 2011, the last reflector panel was installed on 3 July 2016, and the telescope was completed on 25 September 2016. Our Guizhou colleagues work with data from FAST.

- **Formatted address:** MV34+4MC Unnamed Road, Ping Tang Xian, Qian Nan Bu Yi Zu Miao Zu Zi Zhi Zhou, Gui Zhou Sheng, China, 558305
- **Latitude:** 25.6528077
- **Longitude:** 106.8566259
- **Coordinates (as returned by the geocoding lookup):** 25.6528077, 106.8566259
- **Dish diameter:** 500 m (1,640 ft)
- **Completed:** 2016 (last panel installed 3 July 2016; completion announced 25 September 2016)
