# Where We Work — Reference Notes

Reference page for the joint BUPT–Guizhou University research collaboration, listing
the three sites the team works from (Beijing, Guiyang, and the FAST radio telescope
in Pingtang County, Guizhou). Coordinates were obtained from a geocoding lookup
and every background fact below is backed by the source URL listed at the end of
each entry.

## 3. FAST radio telescope (Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope)

**Canonical address (formatted address from geocoding):**
MV34+4MC Unnamed Road, Ping Tang Xian, Qian Nan Bu Yi Zu Miao Zu Zi Zhi Zhou, Gui Zhou Sheng, China, 558305

**Coordinates (from geocoding lookup):**
- Latitude: 25.6528077
- Longitude: 106.8566259

**Background facts:**
- **Dish diameter:** FAST has a **500 m** (1,640 ft) diameter dish, the world's
  largest single-dish radio telescope.
- **Completion:** FAST was **completed in 2016** (main body finished July 2016;
  the telescope was formally commissioned and opened to science on 25 September
  2016) in the Dawodang depression (大窝凼洼地) in Pingtang County, Guizhou.
- Sources: [FAST — Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical radio Telescope — Chinese
  Academy of Sciences](https://fast.bao.ac.cn/) ("FAST... is the world's largest
  single-dish radio telescope, with a receiving area equivalent to 30 football
  fields."); [Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-hundred-meter_Aperture_Spherical_Telescope)
  ("FAST has a 500 m (1,640 ft) diameter dish..."); also the Chinese Academy of
  Sciences project page: [500米口径球面射电望远镜（FAST）工程](https://www.cas.cn/zt/kjzt/fast/)
  ("FAST于2016年9月25日落成启用" — completed and opened on 25 September 2016).
