# Where We Work — Site Reference Page

This page documents the three locations central to our joint Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) – Guizhou University research collaboration: our main campus in Beijing, our main campus in Guiyang, and the FAST radio telescope site in Pingtang, Guizhou (where our Guizhou colleagues do data work).

It is published as a pull request so the team can review and sign off before it goes on the project site.

---

- **Canonical address:** No. 10 Xitucheng Road, Haidian District, Beijing, China (北京市海淀区西土城路10号)
- **Coordinates (from geocoding lookup):** latitude `39.9984`, longitude `116.3632`
- **Formatted address (as returned by the lookup):** `北京市海淀区西土城路10号, Haidian District, Beijing, China` (reverse-geocoded display: Beijing, Haidian District, China; 39.9984, 116.3632)
- **Background fact:** BUPT was founded in **1955**. It is a prominent telecommunications and information-tech university in China.
  - Source: [BUPT Official Website (English)](https://en.bupt.edu.cn/)

## 3. FAST Radio Telescope (China Sky Eye) — Pingtang County, Guizhou

- **Canonical address:** FAST Central Observatory, Pingtang County, Guizhou, China (平塘县，贵州省)
- **Coordinates (from geocoding lookup):** latitude `25.398`, longitude `106.954`
- **Formatted address (as returned by the lookup):** `Pingtang County, Guizhou, China` (reverse-geocoded display: Pingtang County, Guizhou, China; 25.398, 106.954)
- **Background facts:** FAST (the Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope, "China Sky Eye") has a dish diameter of **500 meters** and was **completed in 2023**. It is the largest single-dish radio telescope in the world, and our Guizhou colleagues work with its data.
  - Source: [FAST Official Website](https://fast.bapc.cn/)
  - Source: [Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-hundred-meter_Aperture_Spherical_Telescope)
