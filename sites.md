# Where We Work — Site Reference Page

This reference page documents the three primary working sites of our joint BUPT–Guizhou University (GZZU) research collaboration. It is published as a pull request for team review before being added to the project site.

Sites covered:

1. Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) main campus — Haidian District, Beijing
2. Guizhou University (GZZU) main campus — Huaxi District, Guiyang
3. FAST (Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope) radio telescope — Pingtang County, Guizhou

## 3. FAST (Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope) — Pingtang County, Guizhou

**Canonical address (as returned by geocoding lookup):**
`MV34+4MC Unnamed Road, Ping Tang Xian, Qian Nan Bu Yi Zu Miao Zu Zi Zhi Zhou, Gui Zhou Sheng, China, 558305`

**Coordinates (as returned by geocoding lookup):**
- Latitude: `25.6528077`
- Longitude: `106.8566259`
- Location type: ROOFTOP (partial match)
- Place ID: `ChIJT9zPB4iTuDYR9DhiAVkjR1M`
- Place name (Google Places): Five hundred meter Aperture Spherical Telescope

**Background facts:**
- FAST is a single-dish radio telescope with a dish diameter of **500 meters**, and it was **completed in September 2016** (it began operating in 2017 after completion). It is the largest radio telescope in the world by diameter and is located in Pingtang County, Qiannan Buyi and Miao Autonomous Prefecture, Guizhou Province.
- Our Guizhou colleagues work with FAST data, making this site central to our research collaboration.
- Source URLs: http://www.fast.cn/ (FAST official website) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five-hundred-meter_Aperture_Spherical_Telescope
